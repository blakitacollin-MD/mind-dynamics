A clear, measurable process

From performance questions to practical evidence.

We help athletes and their support teams define what matters, track it consistently, and make decisions using performance data instead of guesswork.

01

Observe

Identify specific behaviors connected to the athlete’s goals, such as routine follow-through, attention shifts, execution consistency, or recovery after an error.

02

Measure

Establish a baseline and track meaningful measures over time. Combine direct observation with the athlete’s own account of focus, confidence, or pressure.

03

Guide intervention

Use the findings to select and evaluate individualized strategies. Review the data to understand what is helping and where to adjust.

Not riding coaching. Behavioral performance science.

Mind Dynamics does not teach riding technique or provide riding instruction. The service focuses on the science of behavior: measuring athletic and mental performance, identifying patterns, and using the data to guide interventions that help an athlete succeed. We can complement an athlete’s existing coaching and care team while staying focused on behavior and performance measurement.
